and redistribution. It does not grant commercial or profit-making use.

If you want to use this project commercially, ask Acidfang through the
repository's issue tracker and describe the specific use.

The intent is not to collect indefinitely merely because this work exists.
Any payment to Acidfang should correspond to direct help, permission, or work
provided for that particular occasion and agreed at that time. Permission for
one commercial use does not automatically authorize a different commercial
use, and no recurring royalty is implied unless it is separately agreed.
