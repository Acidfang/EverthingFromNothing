Its screen position is fixed at the previously used lower default:81.6% of viewport height. Pan, zoom, yaw, pitch and roll move the addressed objects, not the horizon. A HOMEBASE landmark on the well opens the existing user-root sourceAddress and revision. It creates no second home identity and no engine transaction. The well's retained-frame Replay/Observe/NEXT operation remains separate. During sowing, the landmark remains visible, side buckets clear the ground target, and the seed mirror is projected away from the well. Admissibility still comes from retained Guide conditions; screen placement does not establish physical feasibility or completion.

These are viewport presentation choices, not new canonical model geometry.
