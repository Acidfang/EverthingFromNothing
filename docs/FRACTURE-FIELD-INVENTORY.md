## Reading boundary

- Parent links are causal/support dependencies. They do not turn pre-temporal `⇒` into elapsed sequence.
- `GIVEN`, `DERIVED`, `SELECTED`, `GENERATED`, and `UNRESOLVED` describe the bounded claim. They do not mean deployed, live, externally verified, or physically identified.
- **WAS / IS / NEXT is the universal frame at every field/node.** CAN BE and other classifications refine NEXT rather than replacing it. Each JSON entry carries retained source (WAS), its scoped supported claim (IS), and its open remainder without claiming a new act (NEXT).
- `IS` is established current state; `WAS*` is evidence-constrained recovery; `CAN BE*` retains forward branches. Computed or proposed `NEXT` does not become `IS` by being listed.
- Formal symbols and source relations take precedence over this prose and over JSON/URL field names. `ZERO`, absence, potential and a selected coordinate origin are not silently collapsed.
- The nonempty First Difference is explicitly GIVEN in the selected provenance. This inventory does not claim to prove it from literal absence.
- Source citations point to retained repository technical content or the bounded technical excerpts introduced with this inventory.
