# Model-Atom Search

## Search rule

The search does not begin with an image or an externally named particle.

At every generated moment it:

1. extracts every radius-1 local field centred on an active address;
2. canonicalizes the field under all 48 cube rotations and reflections;
3. retains the complete `WAS`, `IS`, and calculated `WILL BE` chronology;
4. permits at most one Face-step between the local centres;
5. requires the `WAS` pattern to return cube-equivalent in `WILL BE`;
6. records the intervening `IS` resolution and six-observer signature.

The internal definition is:

```text
model atom =
  smallest nontrivial local field
  that closes WAS -> IS -> WILL BE
  under cube symmetry and declared local transfer
```

## First result

The first exact invariant appears at moment 4:

```text
5 Differences -> 1 Difference -> 5 Differences
```

The five-Difference `WAS` field is cube-equivalent to the `WILL BE` field. The
intervening `IS` field contains one Difference one Face-step away.

All six observers receive five addresses:

```text
5:5:5:5:5:5
```

The closure spans two moments and its observed recurrence-step greatest common
divisor through the tested range is:

```text
2
```

The result is generated by exhaustive enumeration of the declared local search,
not selected by visual resemblance.

## Interpretation boundary

This establishes a **model atom**: an exact chronologically closed invariant of
the selected recurrence.

It does not by itself establish identity with a currently observed chemical or
subatomic object. Such an external relationship requires a mapping between the
model invariant and the raw observation being compared.
