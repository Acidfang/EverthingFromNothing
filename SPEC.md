## Object boundary and presentation boundary

The selected `Whole` or addressed event is the Grain. The page, panel, scene,
terminal, and other presentations are observers of that Grain; they do not
define its identity or silently enlarge its causal boundary.

Before presentation, the caller declares the relational shape it requires:
the selected centre, finite query radius, and the `INWARD`, `OUTWARD`,
`SPATIAL_FACE`, `WAS`, and `NEXT` relations reached within that boundary.
`queryField` returns that complete addressed relation set and its expandable
frontier. A presentation consumes the returned state. It must not discover
additional object relations while rendering.

Thus a hidden per-item lookup is not merely a rendering cost. It is evidence
that the selected object crossed its declared Grain boundary to obtain State
that its caller did not include. A caller that combines several objects must
compose and deduplicate their declared relation requirements before
presentation; relations outside the declared finite boundary remain visible as
frontier, not silently fetched State.
